Privacy Policy — LastPack

LastPack is developed and published by Mallouk 1416 (the developer named on the Google Play listing).
Contact: lalanaswa0@gmail.com
Effective date: October 8, 2026

The short version

LastPack has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your quit data (quit date, habit amounts, prices, logs, goals) is stored only on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your setup answers: product type (cigarettes, vaping, or nicotine pouches), quit date/time, daily amount, price, currency.
  • Your craving and lapse logs, milestones, treat goal, and settings.

This data never leaves your device except through the export feature, which you control: if you export a backup file and share it somewhere, that copy is your responsibility.

Third-party services inside the app

  • Google Play Billing processes any purchase (subscriptions or the one-time unlock). Payment details are handled by Google; we never see your card information.
  • Google AdMob (only in the free version, only on secondary screens) may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's own privacy documentation. Premium removes ads.
  • Text-to-speech uses your device's built-in speech engine.

Your control

  • Delete everything at any time: Settings → Delete all my data, or uninstall the app.
  • No analytics account exists on our side to delete, because we never receive your data.

Health disclaimer

LastPack is a self-tracking and motivation tool. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and does not replace professional medical advice. Health timeline information is general educational information based on publicly available public-health sources. For help quitting, consult a health professional or your local quitline (US: 1-800-QUIT-NOW).
