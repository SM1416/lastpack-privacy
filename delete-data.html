Where your data lives

LastPack has no accounts and no servers. Everything the app stores — your quit date, habit amounts, prices, craving and lapse logs, goals, and settings — is stored only on your own device. The developer never receives a copy, so there is no server-side data to delete.

How to delete everything

  1. Open LastPack.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete all my data and confirm. This permanently erases the app's data from your device.
  3. Alternatively, uninstall the app — uninstalling also removes all of its on-device data.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you exported a backup file from the app and saved or shared it somewhere, delete that copy as well — exported files are under your control.

Third-party data

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing under Google's own policies.
